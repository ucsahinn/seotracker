Connecting to Google

To read Search Console and Analytics 4 data, get a one-time access key from Google, paste it into the app and choose your site. Step by step.

The app's interface is in Turkish. This guide gives every button and screen name in Turkish with its English meaning in brackets.

By the end of this page the app will be connected to Google on your behalf and will read your site's data. For this, you get a "Google access key" from Google once. It takes about ten minutes, and you never have to do it again. There is also a shorter way (a service account), at the end of the page. That shorter way is the recommended one for most people.

What you need

  • A site verified in Search Console (a property). If it is not verified, the app cannot see it in the list.
  • For Analytics 4, at least "Viewer" permission on that property. This is optional. Search Console alone is enough.
  • A Google account. Google Cloud Console (Google's developer page) is free and does not ask for a card.
  • The app open. You will copy the two redirect addresses (the addresses Google sends you back to the app from after you give permission) from it.

Steps

  1. In the app, open Ayarlar → Google (Settings → Google). You see two addresses. One ends with /api/gsc/oauth/callback (Search Console), the other with /api/ga4/oauth/callback (Analytics). In the Windows package, the addresses start with http://127.0.0.1:3001. On Docker, they start with http://localhost:3001. Note both of them somewhere.
  2. In the browser, go to console.cloud.google.com. In the project picker at the top, choose Yeni proje (New project). The name does not matter. You can write "seowatcher".
  3. Open the API'ler ve Hizmetler → Kitaplık (APIs & Services → Library) page. Type Google Search Console API in the search box, click it and choose Etkinleştir (Enable). If you will connect Analytics too, do the same for the Google Analytics Admin API and the Google Analytics Data API.
  4. Open the API'ler ve Hizmetler → OAuth izin ekranı (APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen) page. Choose Harici (External) as the user type. Write any name you like for the app name, and your own email address in the email fields. The publishing status can stay "Test".
  5. On the same page, add your own Google account under Test kullanıcıları (Test users). Google does not need to approve anything else.
  6. On the API'ler ve Hizmetler → Kimlik bilgileri (APIs & Services → Credentials) page, choose Kimlik bilgisi oluştur (Create credentials), then choose OAuth istemci kimliği (OAuth client ID). This is your Google access key.
  7. Choose Web uygulaması (Web application) as the application type.
  8. Under Yetkilendirilmiş yönlendirme URI'leri (Authorized redirect URIs), add the two addresses from step 1. Do not put a slash at the end. A one-character difference gives a "redirect_uri_mismatch" (address does not match) error in Google.
  9. Choose Oluştur (Create). Google shows a client ID and a secret key.
  10. Go back to the app. In Ayarlar → Google (Settings → Google), paste the ID that ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com into the İstemci kimliği (Client ID) field. Paste the key that starts with GOCSPX- into the Gizli anahtar (Secret key) field.
  11. Choose Kaydet (Save). A "Google erişim anahtarı kaydedildi" (Google access key saved) notice appears. You do not need to restart. If you paste the ID into the wrong field, the app warns you: "Kimlik .apps.googleusercontent.com ile bitmeli. Gizli anahtarı değil, istemci kimliğini yapıştırın." (The ID must end with .apps.googleusercontent.com. Paste the client ID, not the secret key.)
  12. If you have no project yet, create one first with Yeni proje (New project). See Projects.
  13. Go to your project: Proje ayarları → Bağlantılar (Project settings → Connections). On the Google Search Console card, choose Bağlan (Connect).
  14. On Google's permission screen, pick the account you added as a test user and give permission. When you return to the app, a "Search Console bağlandı" (Search Console connected) notice appears.
  15. From the Mülk seç (Choose property) list, pick your site and choose Mülkü kaydet (Save property). If you pick a domain property, the card says "Alan adı mülkü: tüm alt alan adlarını ve http/https adreslerini kapsar." (Domain property: covers all subdomains and http/https addresses.)
  16. For Analytics, on the same screen, choose Bağlan (Connect) on the Google Analytics card, give permission and pick the property. The two connections ask for separate permission. One does not cover the other.

A shorter way: a service account

A service account is a special account that connects to Google in your place. You do not need the consent screen. This path is the recommended one for most people. The OAuth path above is for those who prefer signing in with their own Google account.

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and open IAM & Admin → Service Accounts (in a Turkish console: IAM ve Yönetici → Hizmet hesapları). Make sure the same Cloud project has the APIs from step 3 enabled.
  2. Click Create service account. Give it a name, for example "seowatcher". Skip the role steps and click Done.
  3. On the new account's row, open Keys → Add key → Create new key, choose JSON and confirm. A file downloads. Keep it private.
  4. In seowatcher, open Ayarlar → Hizmet hesabı (Settings → Service account). Paste the contents of the whole file and save. The screen shows the service account's e-mail address. Copy it.
  5. In Search Console, open Settings → Users and permissions → Add user. Enter that e-mail address and choose the permission Full.
  6. For Analytics, in Google Analytics open the property's Admin → Access management. Add the same e-mail address as Viewer.

If a service account is saved, the app prefers it. The "Şu an kullanılan" (Currently in use) line on the Ayarlar (Settings) screen tells you which one is active.

Check

  • On the Proje ayarları → Bağlantılar (Project settings → Connections) cards, the badge should say Bağlı (Connected).
  • The four number cards on the Panel (Dashboard) screen should fill in within a few seconds. On a new site, the numbers can be zero. This is not a connection error.
  • The badge in Ayarlar → Google (Settings → Google) should say Kayıtlı (Saved).

Frequently asked questions

Where is the secret key stored? In the database on this computer, encrypted. The key that encrypts it is the instance-secret file (in the data folder on Windows, in the data volume on Docker). The key is never sent to the browser.

Can I use the same access key in more than one project? Yes. One per installation is enough. Each project picks its own property.

The connection dropped after a few days. For apps in "Test" status, Google can limit the permission to seven days. The card says "Bağlantının süresi doldu" (The connection has expired). Yeniden bağlan (Reconnect) is enough. The lasting fix is to move the consent screen to "Production" or to switch to a service account.

What permissions does it ask for? Read-only for Search Console (webmasters.readonly) and read-only for Analytics (analytics.readonly). The app cannot change anything in Google.

If something goes wrong

  • Google says "redirect_uri_mismatch". The redirect address in Cloud Console is not exactly the same as the one in the app. Use the copy button on the Ayarlar (Settings) screen. http versus https, 127.0.0.1 versus localhost, or a slash at the end is enough to cause the error.
  • A "Google erişim anahtarı eksik" (Google access key missing) warning appears. Steps 10 and 11 were skipped, or saving failed. On the Ayarlar (Settings) screen, the badge should say Kayıtlı (Saved).
  • Google says "access_denied". The account you signed in with is not in the test users list on the consent screen. Add the account to the list, or pick the right account.
  • The property list is empty. This account has no verified site in Search Console and no Analytics permission. Verify the site first.
  • It says "Bağlantısı tamamlanmadı" (Connection not completed) and "state_mismatch". You opened the permission screen in two tabs, or you waited more than ten minutes. Try again from the start in a single tab. If browser cookies (small record files) are off, turn them on.
  • It says "Google hesabı zaten bağlı" (Google account already connected). The account was connected to another record earlier. Remove the old one with Hesabı kaldır (Remove account) in the property picker and connect again.
  • The connections dropped after an update. The encryption key changed (on Docker, BETTER_AUTH_SECRET was added or the data volume was recreated). Choose Yeniden bağlan (Reconnect) on the cards.
