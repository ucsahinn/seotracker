It reads your data from Google and sends it nowhere.

The app opens only from your own computer. It connects to just three places: Google, the pages of the site you audit, and GitHub to check for updates. Your data goes nowhere else. Your Google access key is stored encrypted on your computer.

It turns Search Console numbers into screens that tell you what to do.

These are the app's real screens. Only the site and the numbers are examples.

01 Opportunities

Which page should I work on this week?

It sorts the pages Google shows into four groups: rarely clicked, close to rising, already at the top, and buried deep. What to do is written next to each page, so there is no guessing. Opportunities guide.

02 Site audit

See what is broken on your site before Google tells you.

It walks your site page by page and checks it against 95 rules: broken links, missing or overlong titles, slow pages, pages Google has not indexed. Under every finding it says what to do, no technical help needed. Site audit guide.

03 Search performance

Sixteen months of clicks, impressions, click rate and position.

Look at it by query, page, country and device. The places where two of your pages compete for the same query are collected on their own tab. Search performance guide.

04 AI assistant

Connect an AI assistant if you want.

Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can read the app's data. There are ready-made tasks for the audit, a drop investigation, the monthly check-in and the report. The setup text is waiting on screen; copy it and paste it into your assistant. If you never connect one, the app loses nothing. Agent setup guide.

What it doesn't do

  • It does not estimate search volume. Impression counts come from Google. (Coming soon, free: approximate demand from impressions.)
  • It doesn't count the sites that link to you (backlinks).
  • It doesn't track competitor rankings. It only reads your own site.
  • It does not run in the cloud; one user, one computer. (Coming soon, free: opening it from a phone or tablet on your home network.)

Three steps to install.

  1. Run the installer

    Open the file and press Install. The app sets itself up and opens in your browser. After that you open and close it from the taskbar icon. Installation guide.

  2. Connect to Google

    This is the longest step, but you do it only once. You get an access key from Google; the app shows every step on screen. Paste the key into the Ayarlar (Settings) screen and you are done. Step-by-step guide.

  3. Create your project

    Pick your site. The dashboard, search numbers, opportunities and Google's index status fill in on their own. You start the audit with one button.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It is an MIT-licensed, open source project. All of the code is on GitHub. The Google services it uses stay within their free usage limits.

Why does Windows warn about an "unrecognized app"?

The installer does not have a digital signature yet, so Windows warns you. On the SmartScreen screen, click "More info", then "Run anyway". If you like, compare the file's fingerprint (SHA-256) with the value on the release page. Troubleshooting.

Where is my data kept?

In the %LOCALAPPDATA%\seowatcher folder. The database, your Google access key and the logs are kept there. Uninstalling the app leaves this folder alone. To make a backup, just copy the folder.

Why do I have to create an access key at Google?

seowatcher is not a cloud service. It runs on your computer and connects to Google directly. Google wants to know who owns the app that is connecting. You do this once. The app shows the steps on screen.

Are the numbers the same as in Search Console?

The numbers come from the same source, but the totals may not match. Google delivers data about three days late and hides rarely searched queries. The app shows the page total and the site total separately. A difference between the two is not a bug.

Do I need to make the app reachable from the internet?

No. The app opens only from your own computer. It reads Search Console and Analytics data from Google. During an audit it crawls only the pages of the site you are auditing.

Install it today, see your history today.

Search Console's sixteen months of history load into the app once you connect. You can look the same day.
