Privacy and cookies

This notice covers the seowatcher.io website. Project data in the installed seowatcher application is stored separately on your own computer.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Google Analytics 4 (when enabled)

Google Analytics 4 runs on this site only when a GA4 measurement ID is configured. Without one, the pages send no request to Google, show no cookie banner and store nothing in your browser. Even then, the Google tag (gtag.js) loads only after you choose Accept. What is measured after acceptance: page views and GA4 enhanced measurement events (such as scrolls, outbound clicks and file downloads), together with the page address, referring address, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Google states that GA4 does not log or store IP addresses. We use this to improve the website and the installation guides.

Consent

We use basic Google Consent Mode v2: every analytics and advertising permission starts as denied, and until you consent nothing is loaded from Google and no data is sent to Google. Choosing Accept grants analytics storage only, loads the Google tag and writes the analytics cookies (_ga and names starting with _ga_). Advertising permissions are never granted; ad personalization and Google signals are off.

Retention

Your acceptance or refusal is stored in this browser’s local storage as seowatcher-consent-v1 for 6 months, after which you are asked again. Analytics cookies last at most 13 months.User and event data retention in the GA4 property follows that property's settings. Google's aggregated reports may follow different retention rules. Google’s aggregated reports may follow different retention rules.

Withdrawing consent

Consent is optional; refusing does not stop you using the site or downloading the app. While analytics is enabled, every page has a Cookie settings button at the bottom: use it to change your choice or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing stops measurement, deletes accessible analytics cookies and reloads the page; after that no data is sent to Google. Clearing this site’s data in your browser also resets your choice.

Providers and contact

The site is hosted on a server at Hetzner; the web server processes connection information such as your IP address and the requested URL to serve your request. When analytics is enabled, Google processes Analytics data on our behalf as a processor under Google’s data processing terms, which may include servers outside your country. For privacy or data requests, use the contact channel on the project’s GitHub profile. The Google links below explain further processing and cookie details.
